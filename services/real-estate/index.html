Agent Questions

Everything you need to know.

How do I share photos with my clients?

All photos are delivered via Pixieset, a professional listing platform. You get a shareable link to send directly to your clients, post online, or use in your MLS listing. Easy to download, print, or share on social media.

Can you handle multiple listings per week?

Yes. We can do up to 6 listings per day. Our contract tiers (5/month or 10/month) are designed for agents who have consistent volume. If you need more flexibility, we can discuss custom arrangements.

What if the weather is bad?

Interior photography happens rain or shine. If weather prevents drone shots or exterior coverage, we reschedule those specific parts for the next available clear day—no additional charge. Interior photos are still delivered on schedule.

Do you do commercial properties?

Yes. Commercial properties, industrial spaces, and large acreage are all welcome. Pricing may vary based on square footage and complexity. Contact us for a custom quote.

How fast is turnaround really?

Standard turnaround is 24-48 hours from the shoot date. Edited photos, floor plans, and all deliverables are ready to list. We guarantee this timeframe—if we miss it, your session is free.

What's included in the package?

HDR bracketed photos (30+ edited images covering interior and exterior), professional drone aerial shots, an interactive 2D floor plan with room measurements and a photo tour, and MLS-ready delivery. Everything a property needs to stand out.
