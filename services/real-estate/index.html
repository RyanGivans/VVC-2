Agent Questions

Everything you need to know.

How do I share photos with my clients?

All photos are delivered via Pixieset. You receive a shareable gallery link and can download the MLS-ready files for listing use, social media, or client sharing.

Can you handle multiple listings per week?

Yes. Multiple listings can be scheduled in the same week, and recurring-agent packages are available for consistent volume.

What if the weather is bad?

Interior photography can still move forward. If weather prevents exterior or drone coverage, those portions can be rescheduled for the next workable day.

Do you do commercial properties?

Yes. Commercial properties, industrial spaces, and acreage are welcome. Pricing may vary based on square footage and complexity.

How fast is turnaround?

Standard listing-media delivery is typically 24-48 hours from the shoot date. Weather-dependent exterior and drone work may require a separate clear-weather window.

What's included in the listing package?

The standard $335 package includes HDR interior and exterior photos, professional drone aerial images, an interactive 2D floor plan tour, and MLS-ready delivery.
